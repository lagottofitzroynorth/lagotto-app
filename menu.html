Victorian black truffle 3g add-on
$15

Fresh Victorian black truffle, shaved at the table — 3g per serve. Suggested over the stracciatella, bavette tartare and Jerusalem artichokes, both steaks (the Black Opal wagyu rump and the Robbin's Island oyster blade) and the tiramisu. A truffle upgrade on the brioche can also be offered verbally for $7.50. The celeriac lasagne and the parmesan gelato already come with truffle, so no add-on is needed there. Victorian truffle season runs through winter, which is why it's on the menu now.

Sourdough focaccia, whipped vegemite butter
$14

The dough uses a small amount of fresh yeast and a four-hour process of stretch, fold and rest before the dimples are pressed in, the dough is lathered in olive oil and rosemary and baked. The slower, more natural sourdough fermentation gives the bread its spring, and potato in the dough retains moisture for a softer, chewier crumb. The butter is browned, strained and smoked, then mixed with vegemite and whipped for service, with caramelised milk solids folded back in for texture.

Robbin's Island grass-fed 300g 9+ wagyu oyster blade, rosemary, jus
$125

Robbin's Island is a small island off the north-west coast of Tasmania — when the tide is in the island is separated from the mainland, and when it goes out it reconnects. It's considered one of the best wagyu projects in the world thanks to that environment. This is the grass-fed oyster blade, 300g, at 9+ marbling — 9+ is the highest wagyu score in Australia. Oyster blade is cut from the shoulder, a well-worked and deeply flavoured muscle rather than a lean prime cut, served with rosemary and jus.

Parmesan gelato, cheesecake cream, truffle, caramel, popcorn
$25

The gelato is built on a base of buffalo milk and fior di latte with grated 24-month parmesan folded through — no eggs in the gelato itself. The cheesecake is cream cheese, cream, sugar and vanilla bean, baked and then blitzed to a cream. All the whey from the buffalo milk is reduced down and mixed with sugar and bicarb to make the caramel, and the popcorn is caramelised in that same caramel and salted. Served with a biscotti crumb, which can be left off.
